Privacy Policy

App: NEET PYQ Offline: Practice  |  Developer: SajidApps  |  Last updated: 6 October 2026

NEET PYQ Offline: Practice ("the app") is a fully offline previous-year-question practice app for NEET, AIIMS and JIPMER preparation. Your privacy matters, and this app is designed to collect no personal data at all.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no login, no registration, no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. The app does not send your data to any server of ours, because we do not run one.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your own device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your practice progress (attempted, correct and skipped questions).
  • Bookmarks you create.
  • Notes you write on questions.
  • App settings such as your chosen theme and text size.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share something yourself (see section 3). Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. You can also remove notes and bookmarks at any time from the app's Settings screen.

3. Sharing a question (optional)

The app lets you share a question as an image or text with a friend, for example through WhatsApp. This only happens when you tap the Share button. The image is created on your device and handed to the app you pick through Android's standard sharing feature. The app does not see or store who you share with, and we receive nothing. Once you send content through another app, that app's own privacy policy applies. To limit bulk copying of the question bank, the app keeps a small local counter of recent share times on your device; it is not sent anywhere.

4. Google Play updates

If you install the app from Google Play, the app may use Google Play's in-app update feature to check whether a newer version is available. This check is performed by Google Play services and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The app itself does not receive any personal information from this process.

5. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal data. All questions, explanations and diagrams are bundled inside the app and work in airplane mode.

6. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising networks, analytics tools or social-media SDKs.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for students preparing for competitive exams. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, no information is collected from children either.

8. Content disclaimer

The app is an independent educational practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the National Testing Agency (NTA), AIIMS, JIPMER or any government body. Exam names are used only to describe the question papers practised.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
